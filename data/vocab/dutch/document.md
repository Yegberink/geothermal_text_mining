# Dutch Language Resources

This folder contains Dutch-specific vocabulary and normalization resources used by the workflow.

- `date_locale.yaml`: Dutch month and weekday names for Lexis-style date conversion.
- `geothermal_patterns.txt`: Regex patterns used for preprocessing geo-hit counts.
- `location_province_overrides.csv`: Manual mapping from ambiguous extracted locations to Dutch province names.
- `keywords_topics.csv`: Dutch frame/category keyword vocabulary used by the paragraph filter and sentence/category classifier.
